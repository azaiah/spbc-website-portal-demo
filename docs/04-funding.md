# NLDT funding landscape (verified Oct 19 2026 unless marked VERIFY)

Two applicant entities:
- CHURCH = New Life Deliverance Tabernacle (house of worship; auto-exempt). Eligible for security grants, elective pay, sacred-places programs, corporate matching.
- CENTER = New Life Community Center Nonprofit Organization, EIN 83-4614645, 501(c)(3) since Oct 2022, revenue ~$110K (990-EZ FYE Jul 2025). Use for government & foundation grants that exclude houses of worship (e.g., Ujima). Needs SAM.gov UEI for federal pass-through.
