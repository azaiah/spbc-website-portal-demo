## History (verbatim-derived)
- 1985: Rev. Berkley Jones, Chaplain of Pocahontas Correctional Field Unit #13, dreamed of a church for re-entry citizens with a holistic approach (finances, substance abuse, family relations, employment counseling, vocational training). He passed before it materialized; shared the dream with Robert A. Winfree and Randolph Burt, Sr.
- 1986 Robert Winfree called; ordained 1991. Randolph Burt Sr. licensed 1993.
- Worked with 919 drug rehabilitation centers; Winfree created after-treatment "Relapse Prevention" → renamed "New Life Maintenance" → became the church.
- March 1997: NLDT born; first service at the Richmond Marriott. Support from Pastor Joe Ellison (Essex Village Community Church), Pastor John Hershman, Pastor Bob Laughlin (Westend Assembly of God), Dr. Dwight Jones.
- ~1998: moved to 5450 Midlothian Turnpike (collaboration with Westend Assembly of God).
- Birthed New Life Family Life and Education Center (non-profit) — "Strive to Stay Alive" alternative education program (suspended students: 1:1 tutoring, family counseling, life skills, violence prevention); Mighty Mentor Program; Education Center; Feed-More Program (Blackwell, Southside, Mosby communities fed); junior church.
- 2007: moved to present location 900 Decatur St.
- Three after-school tutorial programs (Belt Atlantic, Blackwell Elementary, 5450). Cease Fire apprenticeship program; wrap-around grief & loss services; 1:1 trauma counseling; family counseling; grade-promotion prep program; transportation for members & community; youth educational/recreational trips. "3-prong approach: education, information, spiritualization."
- Partners: Richmond School of the Arts, River City Middle, Blackwell Elementary, Westover Hills Elementary; ongoing relationship with Richmond Behavioral Health Authority (RBHA) and the City of Richmond.
- Pastor Winfree adopted George Wythe High, River City Middle, Oak Grove-Bellemeade Elementary; tutoring with computers/laptops for perfect attendance; free preschool; low-cost summer camps; Men's Ministry mentoring; Cable Wiring program graduation; Belt Atlantic Enrichment (Bible study/worship inside the neighborhood); adopted children who lost parents to violence; Praise in the Park; bus to polls; COVID masks/sanitizer.
- Community Center mission: "To provide equal access to programs and services for at-risk youth as they strive to discover, develop, and become the best version of themselves."
- Recent outreach (About page): Teachers Appreciation Day (donuts), Belt Atlantic women's fellowship, Hillside community events, Community Garden (photos of plowing/clearing).
